# Privacy Policy — My Trips – Travel Tracker

**Last Updated: November 16, 2026**

## Overview

My Trips – Travel Tracker is an iPhone and iPad application designed to help users organize trips, manage travel budgets, record expenses, and review spending reports.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

## Information You Enter

The application allows you to enter information including:

- Trip names, dates, and durations
- Travel budgets and expense categories
- Expense amounts, descriptions, vendors, and payment methods

This information is used to provide the application's features.

## Data Storage and iCloud Synchronization

Your information is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData technology.

When iCloud synchronization is enabled, your information is synchronized through Apple's CloudKit service using your private iCloud account.

The developer does not operate a separate server to collect or store your travel information.

## Data Collection and Sharing

The application does not intentionally collect personal information for the developer.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with advertising networks.

The application does not include third-party advertising or tracking software.

## Backup and Restore

The application allows you to export your trip information into a JSON backup file.

You control where these backup files are saved and whether they are shared with others.

The application also provides a Restore Backup feature to recover previously exported information.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You can edit or delete trips and expenses within the application.

Deleting the application from a device does not necessarily delete information previously synchronized with iCloud.

Data stored in iCloud is subject to Apple's iCloud services and account settings.

## Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically designed for children and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application or applicable privacy requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page.
