My Dining - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Overview

My Dining is an application designed to help users organize and maintain personal restaurant information, including restaurant names, locations, cuisine types, contact information, and personal notes.

Data Collection

My Dining does not collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer or advertisers. The application does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Data Storage and iCloud

Information entered into My Dining is stored on the user's device and may be synchronized across compatible Apple devices using the user's private iCloud account. iCloud services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Maps and External Websites

My Dining uses Apple Maps to search for restaurants, addresses, and geographic locations. Information necessary to perform these searches may be processed by Apple.

The application may also open restaurant websites and internet searches. These external services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Backup and Restore

Users may create and restore backup files containing their restaurant information. Users are responsible for the storage and security of these backup files.

Data Deletion

Users may delete restaurant information directly within the application. Users may also manage their iCloud data through their Apple account settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to the application or its features.
