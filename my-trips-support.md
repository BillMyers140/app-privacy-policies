# My Trips – Travel Tracker
## Support and Help

Welcome to the support page for My Trips – Travel Tracker, an iPhone and iPad application for managing travel budgets and expenses.

### Getting Started

My Trips allows you to create trips, establish budgets, record expenses, and review spending reports.

To begin, open the app and tap the **+** button to create your first trip.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I create a trip?**

Tap the + button on the main screen. Enter your trip information and save it.

**How do I record expenses?**

Open a trip and use the expense entry feature to add expenses, including vendor, description, payment method, date, and amount.

**How do I compare my spending with my budget?**

Open a trip and select the Budget vs. Actual report.

**Can I use My Trips on both my iPhone and iPad?**

Yes. My Trips uses Apple's iCloud service to synchronize information between devices signed into the same Apple Account.

**How do I back up my information?**

Use the Export Backup feature to save a JSON backup file. Keep a copy in a safe location.

**How do I restore a backup?**

Select Restore Backup and follow the instructions provided in the app.

Restore is intended for recovery purposes, not routine synchronization. Restore on only one device and allow iCloud synchronization to finish before using another device.

**What if my trips are missing after reinstalling the app?**

First, confirm that you're signed into the correct Apple Account and that iCloud synchronization is enabled. Allow time for your information to synchronize before attempting a restore.

### Troubleshooting

If the app isn't behaving as expected:

1. Close and reopen My Trips.
2. Confirm that your device has an internet connection.
3. Check that you're signed into iCloud.
4. Allow time for synchronization to finish.
5. Avoid restoring a backup until you've checked whether your existing information is available through iCloud.
