My Dining - Support

My Dining is a personal restaurant organizer for iPhone and iPad that helps you maintain your restaurant information at home and while traveling.

Getting Started

  1. Add a city using the plus button.
  2. Select the city and add a neighborhood or area.
  3. Select an area and add your restaurants.
  4. Enter restaurant information, including cuisine, address, telephone number, website and personal notes.

Searching for Restaurants

Use the search features to find restaurants by cuisine or personal notes across the areas of a selected city.

Restaurant Maps

Select the map icon on the restaurant list to display restaurant locations. Restaurants with missing or unrecognized addresses may not appear on the map.

iCloud Synchronization

To synchronize restaurant information between your iPhone and iPad, make sure both devices are signed into the same Apple Account and have iCloud enabled.

Backup and Restore

Use the backup and restore buttons on the main My Dining screen to save or restore your restaurant database using the Files app.

We recommend creating regular backups of your restaurant information.
